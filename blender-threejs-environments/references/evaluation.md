Created 2026-09-10 as nine personal Blender + Three.js skills. This record distinguishes portable helper validation from acceptance of a destination game.

## Independent forward evaluations and fixes

Evaluators used unfamiliar synthetic inputs rather than repeating only the authors' expected cases:

- Performance proposals with mixed timings, altered resolution, failed appearance and consistent synthetic gains were distinguished correctly. CPU-update, fragment-cost and first-use traces led to different investigations. Synthetic data was never called hardware measurement.
- Fresh asynchronous adapters found missed final-callback cancellation, mutation of historical evidence, and disposal failures that skipped settlement. All were fixed, independently rerun and covered by permanent regressions.
- New bridge/contact and prop/GLB fixtures rejected floating or wrong-floor contact, scale/artwork-key drift, changed UV/tangents and unsupported compression. Metadata cannot prove visual lettering registration on different LOD topology.
- Independent GI review found a mirrored no-normal guide convention issue. It was corrected with a regression test; unsupported donor boundaries were tightened.
- New normal fixtures passed 70 finite-difference cases. Paint review exposed the need for separate generated/manual layers and explicit drain context rather than freezing or overwriting a flattened output. That gap was corrected and re-evaluated before installation.
- Discovery review routed ordinary LCP/font work outside these skills and a narrow crate move to direct/contact work. Props now has four focused modules. A downstream lettering conflict was corrected so canonical generated artwork stays authoritative for visual design while the user's wording remains authoritative for spelling.

## Deliberate limits

These are static-environment and prop skills, not a new game engine or a universal importer. Helpers explicitly restrict unsupported meshes/formats. Browser fixtures use a local desktop Chromium instance; they do not establish physical-phone performance. Mask previews are flat diagnostics; generated lettering quality, a destination scene's lighting/BRDF, all input methods and final reference fidelity still require actual delivered-scene inspection.

No image-generation job was needed to author this skill set. The preferred reference-isolation and lettering workflows are documented and behavior-reviewed; they are not falsely presented as a newly completed multi-angle art production test.

## Discovery and maintenance

The coordinator links all eight focused skills. Descriptions and module selectors keep ordinary edits from triggering a complete production workflow. Automatic discovery remains enabled through the standard skill default. User preferences, style and authorization take precedence over examples. No scene-specific dimensions, fixed budgets, fonts, codec choices or measured FPS targets became universal defaults.

The source game and its package/release files were not edited by this skill-installation task. Each helper has one owner; other skills link to it. Retain raw fixtures and failed experiment lessons when revising behavior, and rerun the relevant forward evaluation after substantive changes.
