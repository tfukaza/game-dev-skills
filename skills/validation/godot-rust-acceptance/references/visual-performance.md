# Visual and performance evidence

Run normal and maximum supported scenarios with comparable documented settings. Verify complete ready work and zero pending work rather than relying on a partial screenshot. If the camera appears after scene initialization, initial coarse chunks may finish before LOD observation; wait for desired-stride convergence against the installed camera.

Exercise pan, rotation and multiple zoom scales, including mixed neighboring LODs, edited shared borders, stable render-object counts and unchanged simulation state during camera-only movement. Inspect captures for gaps, stale patches, missing terrain, clipping and readable cost or refusal feedback. Headless assertions cannot prove a seam-free image. Acceptance does not authorize an unrelated art pass.

For timing, disclose hardware and thread count, build profile, warm-up, map or graph size, entity count, normal and edit or recovery mean, p99 and maximum, cold setup, buffer fill and backlog recovery. Preserve outliers and failed earlier runs. A measured fixture is not an all-layout guarantee, and bounded scheduling does not prove that every entity arrived or recovery completed.

Shared Cargo target paths are mutable. Before a coordinated timing window, copy the executable into a unique private artifact directory and record relevant first-party source, lockfile, build-command and executable hashes. Execute that copy without rebuilding and check its hash before and after. Coordinate a bounded start and end with affected owners. Record background load and available thermal evidence without inferring causality or stopping unrelated processes.
